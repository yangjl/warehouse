# Competitive Landscape — Xu Logistics Center

## Purpose

### Substitutes and customer alternatives

| Alternative | Why customers use it | What we have to beat |
|-------------|----------------------|----------------------|
| Door-direct trucking (no transload or bonded stop) | Avoids intermediate handling and storage cost | Total landed cost including dwell, demurrage, and equipment imbalance |
| Bulk vessel (ag) instead of container | Lower per-ton freight at scale | Specialty / IP / non-GMO and pork segments that prefer containerized shipments |
| Self-storage on customer property | Control and simplicity | Capex avoidance and surge capacity for the customer |
| Carrier in-house empty depots | Captive cost recovery | Independence, flexibility, and faster turnaround |

## Positioning Notes

- **Current positioning hypothesis**: Xu Logistics Center is the **carrier-neutral, multi-service inland gateway** for Omaha / Council Bluffs, integrating container yard, transloading, general warehousing, and US Customs bonded warehousing on a single 21-acre site, backed by Mr. Xu's 40+ years of ocean-freight relationships and a captive drayage fleet.
- **Evidence supporting the position**:
  - Site adjacency to Bunge, UP, BNSF, and I-29.
  - Owner's depth in ocean freight and an existing trucking company that gives a captive demand and dispatch advantage from day one.
  - Persistent inland container imbalance that favors independent CY operators.
- **Risks or weak claims**:
  - "Carrier-neutral" only works if at least 2–3 carriers actually name the facility; one-carrier exclusivity is also viable but changes the model.
  - Bonded warehouse approval (CBP) and / or FTZ subzone activation has a long lead time and is not guaranteed.
  - Larger national 3PL operators could enter the same market with deeper balance sheets.
  - Class I railroads' policies on third-party depots can change.
- **Next validation step**:
  - Confirm the active third-party CY and warehouse operator list in CB / Omaha with Mr. Xu and a local broker.
  - Confirm FTZ #64 status, current active subzones, and the path to a new subzone.
  - Soft commitments from at least 2 carriers and 2 ag exporters before any equity / debt close.

## Threats To Watch

- A national 3PL (e.g., NFI, GXO, Hub Group, RXO) opens or expands a CB / Omaha footprint.
- UP or BNSF changes inland-depot policies in a way that compresses third-party economics.
- US tariff and trade policy stabilizes at low levels, reducing demand for deferred-duty bonded storage.
- Soybean and pork export volumes drop materially due to trade disputes or a strong USD.
- Iowa property tax abatement programs become less generous in the next legislative session.
