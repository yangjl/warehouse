# Financial Model Notes — Xu Logistics Center

## Purpose

Document the assumptions, inputs, and review status for revenue, cost, margin, growth, capex, and funding scenarios. **All figures below are DRAFT planning estimates, not guarantees.** Human review by Mr. Xu and a CPA is required before any number is used externally.

## Model Scope

- **Planning horizon**: 7 years (Year 0 development → Year 7 stabilized operations).
- **Currency**: USD.
- **Revenue streams**: container yard storage (loaded / empty), gate fees, chassis pool fees, transloading (container ↔ bulk / rail / truck), standard warehouse storage and handling, bonded warehouse storage and handling, captive drayage from Mr. Xu's trucking company, optional value-add services (light M&R, kitting, labeling, fumigation through partner).
- **Cost categories**: land, site work, building shell, racking and equipment, soft costs, labor, fuel and utilities, equipment lease and maintenance, insurance, customs surety bond, property tax (net of abatements), professional services, IT (TOS / WMS / customs interface), marketing, working capital, debt service.
- **Funding assumptions**: blended capital stack of (a) owner equity, (b) USDA / SBA / commercial real-estate loan, (c) equipment financing, (d) outside equity if needed for Phase 2 / Phase 3 acceleration, (e) Iowa and Pottawattamie County / Council Bluffs incentives.
- **Scenario names**: **Base**, **Conservative**, **Upside**. The numbers below represent the **Base** scenario; sensitivity ranges are noted inline.

## Revenue Build (Draft)

The unit economics below are placeholders pending the market-rate survey, customer interviews, and Mr. Xu's calibration.

### Container Yard

- Stabilized slot inventory: 1,000–1,300 TEU.
- Stabilized annual throughput: 60,000–75,000 TEU.
- Blended yield: $4–$7 per TEU per day × average 5-day dwell + $35–$55 per gate move.
- **Year 4 CY revenue range: $5.0M–$8.0M.**

### Transloading

- Stabilized throughput: 3,500–4,500 boxes / year (mix of ag export and import distribution).
- Blended price per container: $400–$600.
- **Year 4 transload revenue range: $1.5M–$2.7M.**

### Standard Warehouse

- 100,000 sq ft modeled; 90% stabilized occupancy by Year 4.
- $0.65 / sq ft / month blended storage + $0.05–$0.10 per case / pallet handling.
- **Year 4 standard warehouse revenue range: $1.0M–$1.6M storage + $0.5M–$1.0M handling.**

### Bonded Warehouse

- 40,000 sq ft modeled; 80% stabilized occupancy by Year 4.
- $0.85 / sq ft / month blended (30% premium vs. standard) + handling and customs service fees.
- **Year 4 bonded revenue range: $0.4M–$0.7M storage + $0.4M–$0.8M services.**

### Captive Drayage And Value-Add

- 30,000–50,000 moves / year at $200–$260 blended (in-radius).
- Pass-through with margin; revenue attributable to the facility entity depends on legal-entity structure between trucking and the facility (decide with CPA).
- **Year 4 drayage and value-add revenue range: $5.5M–$10.0M (gross; allocate accordingly).**

## Funding Ask (Draft, Phase 1 First)

A staged ask matched to phase milestones is more credible than a single large number. Suggested Phase 1 ask:

- **$18M–$24M** for Phase 0 + Phase 1 (site work + container yard + transload + initial equipment + 12 months of operating reserve).
- Phase 2 and Phase 3 funded from a combination of Phase 1 cash flow, incremental debt, and optional equity once carrier and tenant commitments are in writing.

## Sensitivities To Surface In The Model

- **Carrier ramp speed** (months to reach approved-depot status with 2+ carriers) — biggest revenue-timing lever.
- **Soybean / pork export volumes** — directly drives transload revenue.
- **US tariff and trade policy** — drives bonded warehouse demand intensity.
- **Paving spec (asphalt vs. concrete)** — biggest single capex line.
- **Iowa property tax abatement structure and term** — meaningful effect on after-tax IRR.
- **Interest rate on senior debt** — every 100 bps moves DSCR materially.
- **Driver availability and wage inflation** — biggest opex risk.
