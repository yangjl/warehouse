Capture customer interviews, validation conversations, soft commitments, and the gaps that still need to be closed before the plan can be treated as evidence-backed rather than hypothesis-backed.

Everything here is **DRAFT** until the corresponding interview log is added. Mr. Xu's 40+ years of relationships are the discovery engine; the goal of this file is to convert that tacit knowledge into a citeable, reviewable evidence trail.

## Target Customer Segments

### Segment 1 — Steamship lines and inland equipment teams

- **Who**: ocean carriers and their inland equipment / depot teams (Maersk, MSC, CMA CGM, Hapag-Lloyd, ONE, Evergreen, Cosco, ZIM, HMM, Yang Ming, Wan Hai).
- **Why they buy**: empty-equipment imbalance, dwell penalties at congested ramps, and the need for carrier-named off-dock depots near ag origins.
- **What they need from us**: capacity, gate reliability, condition reporting, M&R triage, EDI 322/323 messaging, audited reefer / hazmat exclusions, and a clear chassis pool plan.
- **Decision maker**: regional inland equipment manager + intermodal procurement.
- **What "yes" looks like**: facility added to the carrier's approved depot list with a published tariff and minimum equipment allocation.

### Segment 2 — Ocean NVOCCs and freight forwarders

- **Who**: bookings-side NVOCCs and forwarders moving Midwest cargo (Expeditors, Kuehne+Nagel, DHL Forwarding, DSV, regional Asia-focused NVOCCs, and the China-origin specialists Mr. Xu has worked with).
- **Why they buy**: flexible CY space and transload labor without long-term commitment; predictable drayage availability.
- **What they need from us**: short-notice capacity, weekend and second-shift availability, accurate billing, and a single point of contact.
- **Decision maker**: branch operations manager.
- **What "yes" looks like**: monthly recurring transload and CY revenue.

### Segment 3 — Ag exporters and originators

- **Who**: Bunge (CB soybean complex, adjacent), ADM, Cargill, CHS, regional crush plants, Tyson, Smithfield, JBS, and the cluster of grain elevators and specialty (non-GMO, organic, IP) soy and corn handlers in Iowa and Nebraska.
- **Why they buy**: containerized export programs (especially specialty and pork) need predictable empty-box supply and reliable transload labor on tight timing. Mixed bulk-vessel + container strategies are increasingly common.
- **What they need from us**: empty-box pool at known cost per box, food-grade transload practice, sealing and chain-of-custody documentation, USDA / FSIS compliance for protein loads, and the ability to surge during harvest peaks.
- **Decision maker**: logistics manager / commercial export desk.
- **What "yes" looks like**: 12-month transload agreement with a baseline number of boxes per week and a peak surge clause.

### Segment 4 — Import distributors

- **Who**: appliance and home goods distributors, e-mobility (e-bikes, e-scooters, EV components), furniture, automotive parts distributors, machinery dealers, and consumer-electronics regional DCs sourcing from East Asia and Mexico.
- **Why they buy**: deferred-duty bonded storage, FTZ-equivalent benefits, regional distribution closer to the upper-Midwest customer base, and a single operator handling drayage, transload, and storage.
- **What they need from us**: clean, CBP-compliant bonded operations; accurate IT (TOS + WMS + customs interface); reliable retrieval SLAs; and the ability to scale racked space.
- **Decision maker**: VP supply chain / director of import logistics.
- **What "yes" looks like**: signed bonded warehouse storage agreement with minimum monthly square footage and in/out handling commitments.

### Segment 5 — Regional 3PLs and brokers

- **Who**: mid-market 3PLs and freight brokers needing overflow space, transload labor, or last-mile dray.
- **Why they buy**: variable-cost overflow capacity in a tight regional market.
- **What they need from us**: published tariffs, fast onboarding, and clean accounting.
- **Decision maker**: operations director.
- **What "yes" looks like**: enrolled-vendor status with recurring spot business.

## Interview Prompts (Use For Every Conversation)

1. Walk me through how your team moves containers (or product) through the Omaha / Council Bluffs / Midwest area today.
2. What does it cost you when an empty box, a chassis, or a slot at a CY is not available where and when you need it?
3. Where do containers, trucks, or product sit longer than you would like? What does that dwell cost you?
4. Who do you use today for off-dock CY / transload / bonded storage in this region, and what works or does not work about them?
5. If we offered [specific service] at [draft pricing] starting in [month], how would you evaluate it? What would have to be true?
6. What does your decision and contracting process look like? Who else would I need to talk to?
7. Would you sign a letter of intent / soft commitment based on what we've described?
