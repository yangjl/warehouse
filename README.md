# Xu Logistics Center — Council Bluffs Container Yard, Transload, and Bonded Warehouse

This repository holds the business plan for an inland container yard, transloading, and warehouse facility on a 21-acre site near Bunge Avenue along Interstate 29, just east of Omaha, Nebraska (Council Bluffs, Iowa).

This is a human-in-the-loop business planning project. Mr. Xu and his team remain responsible for strategy, customer interpretation, market sizing, pricing, financial forecasts, and any external-facing claims. AI assistants help with drafting, organization, validation, data cleanup, documentation, and presentation production.

## Key Site And Operating Facts (To Confirm With Mr. Xu)

- **Site**: 21 acres along Interstate 29, near Bunge Avenue, east of Omaha, NE.
  - Geographically, I-29 and Bunge Avenue place the site in **Council Bluffs, Pottawattamie County, Iowa**. Iowa (not Nebraska) tax, zoning, and incentive frameworks apply. Confirm exact APN, current zoning (likely M-2 or I-2 industrial), and entitlement status.
- **Adjacencies**: Bunge North America's Council Bluffs soybean processing complex; Union Pacific's Council Bluffs Yard; BNSF Gibson Yard and Lincoln intermodal flows; I-29 / I-80 / I-480 interchange; Eppley Airfield (OMA) is roughly 5 miles west.
- **Primary use**: **container yard** (storage of loaded and empty marine boxes, chassis pool, M&R area).
- **Secondary uses**: **transloading** (rail-to-truck, container-to-truck, bulk-to-container — especially for ag exports out of Bunge / ADM / Cargill / CHS feeders) and **warehouse operations** (general dry storage **plus** US Customs bonded warehouse under 19 CFR Part 19, with FTZ #64 subzone status as an alternate path to evaluate).

## Human Review Checkpoints

The following items in this plan are explicitly drafted at low / medium confidence and must be reviewed by Mr. Xu (and, where applicable, by counsel, a customs broker, a civil engineer, and a CPA) before they are used externally:

- market sizing for Omaha / Council Bluffs intermodal volume captured by the facility
- customer mix between steamship lines, ag exporters, import distributors, and 3PLs
- pricing for storage per TEU per day, gate fees, transload per container, warehouse storage, and bonded fees
- capex estimates for site paving, gates, lighting, M&R, racked warehouse, and bonded warehouse buildout
- opex (labor, equipment lease / financing, insurance, customs surety bond, utilities)
- revenue, margin, and IRR projections; payback period; funding ask
- any claim about expected BNSF / UP partnership, FTZ status, or CBP bonded warehouse approval
